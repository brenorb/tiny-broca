## Third-party material

- Text with `source` `cv` comes from the Mozilla Common Voice Brazilian
  Portuguese sentence files (CC0), https://github.com/common-voice/common-voice.
  The `source_id` and `source_url` columns identify each sentence.
- Text with `source` `converse` is generated by this project's deterministic
  conversational slot pools, and text with `source` `templates` is generated
  by its number/date/money templates. Both are written for this project.
- The audio was generated with Qwen3-TTS 1.7B Base (Apache-2.0). The two
  voices are clones of real speakers from Mozilla Common Voice: short CC0
  recordings, used only as voice prompts for the generator. The recordings
  themselves are not distributed as training audio.
